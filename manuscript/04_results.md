## Study flow

<!-- 16a; Figure 1; numbers.md §1; derived/table1.json -->

The 8 hospitals had 44,626 newborn-unit admissions with at least one pSBI sign between January 2021 and December 2024 (Figure 1). After 237 (0.53%) were excluded for missing data, 44,389 remained. Keeping one admission per unique combination of the 14 clinical features left 7,994. Sampling 300 per triage category gave 1,500 records (18.76% of 7,994).

All 1,500 records were used. Of these, 200 records (40 per triage category) formed the clinician evaluation sample and produced 800 review items. The other 1,300 records were eligible for the retrieval ablation:
- 844 were used in the first generation run;
- 136 were added in the top-up run;
- 320 were skipped as near-duplicates.

This gave 980 holdout questions. For 47 of them (4.80%), all in the antibiotic-course area, the answer cited no passage, which left 933 questions for analysis. Of these, 234 were assessment questions and 233 were in each other area. No record was in both samples.

## Record characteristics

<!-- 16b; Table 1; derived/table1.md -->

Table 1 describes the records in each set. In the clinician evaluation sample:
- median gestational age was 38.0 weeks (IQR 32.0–39.0; missing for 18 of 200);
- median birth weight was 2,700 g (IQR 1,598–3,200);
- 117 of 200 (58.50%) were male.

In the 933 analysed ablation records:
- median gestational age was 38.0 weeks (IQR 32.0–39.0; missing for 82, 8.79%);
- median birth weight was 2,680 g (IQR 1,755–3,200);
- 550 (58.95%) were male.

Median day of life was 1 (IQR 1–1) in every set. Each triage category made up 40 (20.00%) of the evaluation sample. In the analysed ablation set, the categories ranged from 170 (18.22%, Critical) to 206 (22.08%, Substantial). Missing data for the first recorded vital signs ranged from 8.79% to 21.93% across signs and sets.

## Pipeline outcomes on the review items

<!-- 16c; derived/review_outcomes.json -->

All 800 answers were generated by the same model (qwen3.6:35b). The pipeline released a cited answer for 760 items (95.00%) and escalated 40 (5.00%); no item returned "no guideline found". By query area, the escalations were:
- 39 of 200 antibiotic-course items (19.50%);
- 1 of 200 assessment items (0.50%);
- none of the investigation or severity-classification items.

By triage category, escalations were 5 of 160 (3.13%) Critical, 8 (5.00%) Substantial, 6 (3.75%) Moderate, 11 (6.88%) Low and 10 (6.25%) Minimal. The 40 escalated items were also the 40 whose answers cited no passage.

## Retrieval ablation (secondary outcome, exploratory)

<!-- numbers.md §5, tables A–H -->

**Gold sets.** The 933 gold sets contained a mean of 6.32 passages (median 6, range 2–14). Together they drew on 51 distinct passages, and there were 488 distinct gold sets. Within each area, the most-cited passage appeared in 89.32% (assessment) to 99.14% (investigations) of gold sets.

**Lexical–semantic weight.** Pooled over the four query-construction conditions, MRR@10 was:
- 0.376 (95% CI 0.358–0.395) with semantic scoring only (w = 0);
- 0.864 (0.849–0.880) with lexical scoring only (w = 1);
- highest at w = 0.8, at 0.866 (0.851–0.881) (Figure 2; Additional file 3, Table S2).

Within each condition, MRR@10 rose by between 0.463 (0.439–0.487) and 0.508 (0.483–0.532) from w = 0 to w = 1 (all p<0.001). The best weight, w = 0.8, chosen post hoc, did not differ from w = 1 (difference +0.002, −0.001 to +0.004; p = 0.190). The highest single value was 0.872 (0.857–0.888), for present findings only, as written, at w = 1.

**Finding handling and enrichment.** Stating present findings only rather than all assessed findings changed MRR@10 by −0.003 (−0.005 to 0.000; p = 0.089). Concept enrichment changed it by +0.010 (+0.005 to +0.014; p<0.001) with present findings only, and by +0.011 (+0.005 to +0.017; p<0.001) with all assessed findings (Additional file 3, Table S3).

**Popularity baseline.** The query-blind baseline reached an MRR@10 of 0.803 (0.785–0.820). Against it, the 24 condition-by-weight combinations fell into three groups (Additional file 3, Table S4):
- **Higher (10 combinations):** all four conditions at w = 0.8 and w = 1, and both enriched conditions at w = 0.6. Differences ranged from +0.053 to +0.070, all p<0.001.
- **No different (2 combinations):** both as-written conditions at w = 0.6 (+0.007; p = 0.512 and p = 0.507).
- **Lower (12 combinations):** every condition at w ≤ 0.4. Differences ranged from −0.438 to −0.100, all p<0.001.

**By query area (exploratory).** Pooled MRR@10 changed with w as follows (Additional file 3, Table S5):
- **Assessment:** 0.716–0.733 at every weight.
- **Investigations:** from 0.244 (w = 0) to 0.945 (w = 1).
- **Severity classification:** from 0.327 (w = 0) to 0.937 (w = 0.6 and 0.8).
- **Antibiotic course:** from 0.199 (w = 0) to 0.866 (w = 1).

Concept enrichment changed MRR@10 by:
- **Antibiotic course:** +0.057 (+0.050 to +0.064; p<0.001) in both finding-handling conditions.
- **Assessment:** −0.008 (p<0.001 with present findings only; p = 0.001 with all assessed findings).
- **Investigations:** −0.023 (p<0.001) with present findings only, and −0.009 (p = 0.145) with all assessed findings.
- **Severity classification:** no difference in either condition (p = 0.073 and p = 0.624).

Finding handling differed only for investigations, at −0.015 (−0.024 to −0.007; p<0.001). For antibiotic course, the two finding-handling conditions produced identical queries, so their difference was 0 by construction.

**Recall@10.** Recall@10 ranged from 0.280 to 0.329 at w = 0 across conditions. It peaked at 0.663 (0.652–0.674), for all assessed findings with enrichment at w = 0.6, and was 0.590–0.638 at w = 1 (Additional file 3, Table S8).

**Sensitivity analyses.** Against the reference passage sets curated by the investigators, MRR@10 was 0.656–0.699 at w = 0 and 0.987–0.999 at w ≥ 0.8 (Additional file 3, Table S6). Across K = 2 to 20, the baseline's MRR rose from 0.787 to 0.803. At w ≥ 0.6, MRR across the four conditions ranged from 0.783 to 0.873. At every K, the same 10 combinations were above the baseline, 12 were below and 2 did not differ (Additional file 3, Table S7).
